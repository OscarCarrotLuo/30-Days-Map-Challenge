# Lunar Contours

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

An interactive lunar topographic sheet for Day 2, Lines, of the 30 Day Map Challenge. Visitors drag and zoom across the Moon to read its relief, contour lines, coordinates, and named places.

## Capabilities and constraints

The geographic grid, scale bar, and footprints on the two lunar hemispheres follow the map. The white margin carries the data sources and the cartographer's credit, Peirong Luo Oscar. The reference is the supplied monochrome printed lunar sheet with fine white grid lines and restrained red markings. Keep the map free of an introductory description and subtitle.

The photographic field is always square. Keep all geographic coordinate text on pure white paper outside the field. Set all visible lettering in one face and use one treatment for named places. Wide screens and shallow landscape phones carry the notes beside the photograph. Portrait and near-square browser views carry them below. Center the square and marginalia as a complete composition. Set the title “LUNAR CONTOUR LINES” in Barlow Bold with “Peirong Luo Oscar” centered directly below it. Omit the masthead, toolbars and on-map navigation buttons. Panning and zooming remain available through direct manipulation and the keyboard.

Fit the square photograph and the complete multi-column marginalia on one screen. Give every visible text element the same proportional drafting face and size. A feature name in the image or its register row flies to its lunar coordinates.
