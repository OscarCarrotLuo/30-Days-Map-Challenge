# Cartographic sheet

The photographic field is square. White contour lines, geographic grid lines and named features follow the terrain. Longitude and latitude are typeset on the surrounding white paper. Screen and canvas lettering share one size of Barlow. The colophon uses Barlow Bold for its uppercase title and Barlow Medium for the byline directly beneath.

The scale has a five-part extension left of zero, alternating fine blocks, and numbered major divisions in kilometres. The scale and the near- and far-side locator footprints are calculated from the live view.

The feature register, locator, scale, data sources and signed title block share two columns, expanding to three when the note area is wide enough. The table uses thin red rules modeled on the supplied printed reference. The chart fits the square image to the space left after measuring the finished notes. Wide windows carry the notes beside the photograph. Portrait and near-square windows use a common vertical centerline for the photograph, scale and colophon. Side-by-side layouts share a horizontal centerline. The geographic axes project into the white border. The signed title spans the complete note grid, and the larger bottom layout uses equal outer tracks around its central scale.

Clicking a feature in the image or the index frames its terrain and centers the lunar coordinate. The feature register uses a fixed order and scrolls vertically beneath its column headings. The opening seven entries retain their original order, followed by the remaining feature names alphabetically. A drag can begin on a feature label. Reduced motion applies the destination immediately.
